| Natural Earth | Land polygons (1:10m) used for the route grids are public domain. |
| Landsat, Sentinel-1, WorldCover and NOAA OISST | Earth Engine provides access but does not replace each dataset's source terms. Follow the licence and attribution links on the dataset catalog pages recorded in the README before publishing derived outputs. |
