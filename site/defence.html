Defence and security organisations

Open Arctic environmental context for planning teams.

Ministries, coast guards, navies, contractors and research units work in the Arctic where sea ice, weather and daylight decide what is possible. This project offers open, reproducible tools for that environmental picture. It is deliberately not a targeting, tracking or surveillance system.

Where it fits

What defence and security teams can use it for.

Environmental awareness

Ice, weather and light

Current station weather, NASA imagery and pan-Arctic sea-ice extent, with daily sea-ice concentration feeding the route planner. Each value states its source and date.

Logistics and exercises

Route and timing estimates

Shortest open-water paths between ports with sailing time, daylight, wind and wave forecasts, and an option to avoid sea ice above a chosen concentration. A first-pass planning aid for sustainment and exercise design.

Assurance

A method you can inspect

Open source under the MIT licence, with configuration in one file, checksummed outputs, tests and a verifier. Teams can review it, reproduce it on their own data and adapt the study area.

Will not do

Boundaries that do not change with the customer.

  • No identifying, tracking or targeting of specific vessels, people or groups.
  • No inference of intent. An AIS gap is an event with an unknown cause; an unmatched radar detection is a detection. Neither is described as suspicious, hostile or evasive.
  • No operational navigation, ice routing or safety-of-life use. Outputs are planning estimates, not certified products.
  • No handling of classified or controlled material. Do not send it through this website or the contact form.

Deploying it

Running it in your own environment.

  • The site is static files. It can be hosted on your own infrastructure, and the route planner runs in the browser, so entered ports and dates are not sent to this project.
  • The live feeds call public services (Environment Canada, NASA, Open-Meteo, OpenFreeMap) from the browser. A restricted network needs those hosts allowed, or the feeds replaced with mirrors.
  • No accreditation, security certification or clearance is claimed. This is an open-source project maintained by one person, with no service level.
